# Coffix App — v1.0.0 Release Notes

**Release Date:** May 2026
**Platform:** iOS & Android
**Market:** New Zealand

## What's New

We're thrilled to launch the first public release of **Coffix** — your go-to coffee ordering app designed for New Zealanders who love their coffee done right.

---

## Features

### Order Ahead, Skip the Queue
Order your favourite coffee and food for pickup in just a few taps. Choose a pickup time that works for you — now, in 15 minutes, or in 30 minutes.

### Coffix Credit
Load up your Coffix Credit balance and pay instantly at checkout — no fumbling for cards. Every top-up comes with a bonus:
- $50+ → 10% bonus credit
- $250+ → 15% bonus credit
- $500+ → 20% bonus credit

### Loyalty Rewards Built In
Credit-based loyalty means every dollar you load earns you more. No punch cards. No fuss.

### Coffix Gift
Send coffee credit to friends and family. If they're not on Coffix yet, they have 30 days to sign up and claim it.

### Referral Program — Coffee On Us
Invite a friend to Coffix. Once they make their first purchase, you both get rewarded. Simple as that.

### Full Menu at Your Fingertips
Browse coffee, pastries, smoothies, and food across all Coffix locations. Customise your order with size, milk, strength, syrup, and temperature options.

### Find Your Nearest Store
View all active Coffix locations, search by name or address, and see real-time open/close status.

### Secure Payments
Pay with Coffix Credit, credit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay — all processed securely via Windcave. No card data is ever stored.

### Order History & Receipts
View your full order history and credit transaction history at any time from your profile.

### Push Notifications
Stay in the loop with order updates, promotions, and store announcements.
